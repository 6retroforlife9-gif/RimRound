JUNK FOOD FOR THE CURRENT RIMROUND REWORK
Changes from Oinky's supplied RimWorld 1.5 archive
1 November 2026

ORIGINAL CONTENT AND DOCUMENTATION

Junk Food remains a separate optional mod by Oinky, using package ID Oinky.JunkFood.
The original 93 foods, artwork, preview and retained sound asset are preserved.
CREDITS.txt retains the original configuration and sound credits and links.
Documentation/Original/About-1.5.xml is a byte-for-byte copy of the original
About.xml, including its description, author, credits and old dependencies.
The supplied archive contained no separate README, manual or licence text.
That archived About.xml is historical documentation, not the active load order.

RIMWORLD AND RIMROUND COMPATIBILITY

- Updated for RimWorld 1.6 and the current RimRound rework with its companion DLL.
- Harmony and RimRound are required. Load Junk Food after RimRound. RimRound
  remains usable without Junk Food, and the add-on keeps its own Mods folder.
- The old Randy Burger dependency is no longer required; native eating sounds
  are used. Do not enable the old Junk Food package alongside this one.
- The foods are ordinary edible food instead of social drugs, so RimRound can
  recognise them for supported self-eating, shared meals, manual and bedside
  feeding and feast food selection. Normal food and ingredient restrictions apply.
- Corrected malformed legacy XML, missing/broken thoughts and recreation references.
- Retained old food/recipe/chemical/gene identifiers for scoped save migration.
  Retired obsolete junk-food addiction states without removing unrelated health
  or chemical states. This does not certify every historical player save.

FOOD, STOMACH SPACE AND WEIGHT

- Preserves or improves the original food energy and calories per stomach space.
  Densities range from 4x to 7.5x an ordinary meal.
- RimRound accounts for useful food intake, digestion and energy balance. The
  add-on does not apply a second instant weight-gain effect on top of that intake.
- All food has at least four days before normal-temperature rot; freezing pauses
  native rot. Opening a package preserves its remaining life and other metadata.
- Snacks share a capped +3 taste memory; savoury meals use the normal fine-meal
  memory. Recreation uses the existing Gluttonous tolerance rather than making
  every brand a separate endlessly repeatable recreation source.

CRAFTING

- Junk Foods research costs 500. Cooking requirements range from skill 3 to 6.
- All 189 production recipes are available at fueled and electric stoves;
  drinks and frozen desserts also have 65 brewery entries.
- Uses ordinary crops, milk, native chocolate and appropriate potatoes/meat/fish.
  No extra flour, sugar, oil or cheese manufacturing chain is required.
- Provides single and bulk production while preserving historical recipe IDs and
  original output counts. Retired medical administration recipes remain inert
  compatibility aliases rather than a second way to generate or administer food.

PACKAGES AND COLLECTING FOOD

- The 90 ordinary foods are ready to eat. Boxes, bags and wrappers in their art
  do not require a separate opening job before a pawn collects or eats them.
- Three larger packages can be opened into individual servings:
    large cola bottle -> 6 individual colas
    whole Pizza Pizza -> 4 slices
    KitKat pile       -> 4 bars
- A hungry colonist choosing food automatically opens one bulk package when
  its servings are allowed by their food policy and there is reachable accepting
  storage nearby. Normal food jobs then collect and eat the actual servings.
- Haulers can prepare a serving stack for hungry residents, including patients,
  so ordinary feeding jobs can collect it. Existing available servings are used
  before another package is opened. This does not unpack the entire stockpile.
- Right-click Open one remains available for manual preparation. That manual
  order leaves servings nearby; normal hauling can put them into storage.
- Packages remain edible if automatic preparation is unavailable. Food already
  reserved or claimed by a feast is not replaced behind that job's back. Existing
  inventory/caravan food and already-planned ritual servings retain their usual
  behavior. No universal interception of every mod's feeding job is claimed.
- Opening conserves nutrition, stomach volume and mass, and transfers age,
  ingredients, poisoning, relative condition and infection ownership.
  Opening does not award eaten calories, weight or craving progress.

OPTIONAL SETTINGS AND SUPPLY

- Enable Junk Food and Enable mild cravings are independent colony-saved options.
- Turning content off stops new production, supply and junk recreation. Existing
  food remains edible and existing packages can still be opened.
- Mild adult cravings replace the old chemical addiction. The optional penalty
  is -2 mood after a qualifying habit and 48 hours without a snack; abstinence
  clears it. Disabling cravings clears the history immediately.
- RimRound food traders carry rotating families of treats; gifts can include
  them within their normal budget. Resource-pod cargo has a scoped 10% junk-food
  branch; the mod does not increase the frequency of pod incidents.
- Junk Food is excluded from RimRound's liquid-food processors because those
  processors cannot currently preserve every food-specific eating effect.

PAIRED RIMROUND UPDATE

- The paired RimRound build enables the Body Perk Tree for new colonies.
  Existing colonies retain their saved choice; no perks or points are granted.
  Disabling the tree still preserves purchases, and Legacy uses its old perks.
- Factory pawn eligibility is ages 18–200, with male, female, hostile, friendly,
  prisoner and slave options enabled. Existing colony choices and user-defined
  new-colony defaults remain respected. The old saved-max200-to1000 conversion
  is removed. Resetting the Pawn eligibility page restores these factory values.
- Missing icons are assigned for nine RimRound item categories. All existing
  food, drug, equipment and body behaviour remains unchanged by that icon fix.
- The refreshed manual showroom has its Body Perk Tree explicitly enabled and
  all six eligibility switches enabled at ages 18–200.

VERIFIED SCOPE

The underlying Junk Food runtime was tested in RimWorld 1.6.4871 with Core and
all five official DLCs using the recorded Harmony, VEF and HAR versions. Native
checks covered actual crafting, manual package opening, individual self-eating,
sharing, manual/bedside feeding, settings, selected legacy migration/reloads and
a five-day finite-pantry comparison. All 93 foods passed the recorded feast
selection predicates; this is not a new full ritual test with this add-on.

The paired update's new automatic-package and default-setting results are
recorded separately in Documentation/Pairing-Test-Results.txt.

Direct whole-pizza/KitKat ingestion jobs, arbitrary mod lists,
caravan consumption, fueled-stove/brewery crafting jobs and RimWorld 1.7 were not
established by those tests. Keep only one copy of each mod and back up a colony
before changing its mod list.
