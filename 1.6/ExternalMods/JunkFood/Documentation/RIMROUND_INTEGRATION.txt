RimRound integration - 1 November 2026

Junk Food is now included inside the RimRound rework. Enabling RimRound supplies
the 93 foods, 189 recipes, Junk Foods research, textures, sounds and existing
automatic package-opening behavior without a separate Junk Food download.

Original content and artwork remain credited to Oinky and the contributors in
CREDITS.txt. The original documentation and earlier update records are retained.

Food definitions, recipe IDs, assembly identity and saved colony settings retain
their existing names. The Junk Food settings page remains available under Mod
options, with the existing content and mild-cravings controls.

When the separate Oinky.JunkFood package is enabled, RimRound skips its embedded
copy so the game loads only one implementation. Use the bundled version for the
current rework. This guard does not update an obsolete standalone package.

The integration does not change food nutrition, stomach volume, ingredients,
weight accounting, research costs or recipe outputs. RimRound continues to own
digestion and body changes. Original saved content remains identified by the
same Def names and component types.
