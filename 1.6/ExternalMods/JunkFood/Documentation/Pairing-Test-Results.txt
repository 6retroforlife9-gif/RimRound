PAIRED RIMROUND / JUNK FOOD FOLLOW-UP TESTS
1 November 2026

Tested on RimWorld 1.6.4871 rev591, with Core alone and with all five official
DLCs. Both profiles included the pinned Harmony, Humanoid Alien Races and Vanilla
Expanded Framework dependencies. Protected copies of the accepted test colony
were used; raids, animals, hostile pawns and other saved test hazards were blocked.

AUTOMATIC PACKAGES

- Actual hunger scheduling chose an opening job, produced stored individual
  servings, and then collected and ate those servings for large cola, KitKat piles
  and whole pizza. Both Maintain and Gain were exercised. Gain consumed four bars
  together, demonstrating real stack pickup and intake.
- A fed hauler independently prepared cola for a hungry pawn in their own bed.
  The existing manual feeding command then collected and fed the opened drinks.
  The AllDlc case served four drinks; the Core case served five. The helper did
  not eat them. This verifies autonomous preparation and actual ordered feeding,
  rather than claiming a new autonomous feeding scheduler.
- Real save/reload during the opening delay resumed the same job, with the
  original package still intact and no duplicated intake or opening transaction.
- Blocking storage or disallowing the child food while opening was underway
  stopped preparation without losing the package. Native eating then used the
  ordinary whole-package fallback. A bounded retry deadline prevented immediate
  reopening loops. Useful intake plus waste matched the food actually removed.
- Direct paused checks covered the 90 unchanged ready-to-eat foods, child food
  policy, native reservations, existing-serving preference and quantity, a
  logical feast claim, storage denial, and the suppressed-food-need boundary.
- Each package pair was checked at source counts one and two, including content
  disabled. Nutrition, stomach volume, mass, remaining rot time, ingredients,
  poison, relative condition and optional Anomaly infection ownership were kept.
  Opening itself awarded no intake credit.

Core testing initially chose a Builder whose native backstory forbids hauling.
The run stopped before that case; its previous food/reload cases had passed.
The remaining case resumed from the saved checkpoint with an existing capable
Cook. No colony was regenerated and no pawn capabilities were changed.

DEFAULT SETTINGS

Production new-game/new-world initializer diagnostics and the actual settings-page
reset gave Body Perk Tree enabled and eligibility ages 18-200/all six switches
enabled. These diagnostics do not claim a full new-colony generation playthrough.
Real saves/reloads retained enabled choices and deliberate saved opt-outs/custom
age limits. An old max200 save remained200. Legacy keeps the new tree dormant.
All ten RimRound category icons resolved to valid native textures.

SCOPE

These focused checks supplement the previous crafting, food accounting, feeding,
legacy migration and pantry tests. The original 93 foods, 189 production recipes,
three package relationships and all 282 supplied art/sound files were verified.
No food balance or recipe change was introduced by this follow-up.

This is not universal compatibility certification. A real feast with this add-on,
automatic opening within already-claimed ritual allocations, caravan opening,
arbitrary mod lists, a VRE Android-specific opening playthrough, a post-commit
opening reload, and RimWorld 1.7 remain outside these focused checks. The bedside
test used a sleeping pawn, not a new downed-pawn scenario. The claim and suppressed
metabolism checks were explicit diagnostics, not full ritual/Android playthroughs.
Whole-cola fallback was played; direct whole-pizza/KitKat ingestion was not.
